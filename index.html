    Three ways in

    1. Connect Spotify. Signs in with OAuth (PKCE, no server), lists your playlists, and lets you save suggestions straight to your account.
    2. Upload a CSV. Export any playlist from exportify.net. Columns are matched by name, and audio feature columns unlock mood, tempo and smart order.
    3. Demo playlist. Fictional tracks with full audio data, so every feature can be explored.

    Under the hood

    • Genre diversity is normalized Shannon entropy over genre tags.
    • Mood comes from the valence and energy quadrant of each track.
    • Archetype match is Euclidean distance between the playlist's mean feature vector and six reference profiles.
    • Suggestions pull candidates from Spotify search seeded by your top artists and genres, then rank them with a weighted score of artist affinity, genre overlap, release era and sound, with a cap per artist for variety.
    • Smart order is a greedy search that follows a target energy curve and penalizes tempo jumps and key clashes.
